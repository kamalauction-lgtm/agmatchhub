## Critical blocker definition (only these interrupt the pilot)

1. An agent cannot sign in, verify or reach their dashboard.
2. A listing cannot be created, published or edited.
3. A lead or enquiry cannot be seen or replied to.
4. A pool pickup, requirement or submission fails or saves wrong data.
5. Any confidential field (commission, minimum price, supply-agent identity, client identity) appears where it must not.
6. Any money figure or commission split is wrong.

Everything else: cosmetic, wording, slow page, missing photo, dark-mode contrast → backlog.

## Success criteria for the week

- 5 agents active on ≥3 days each.
- Every lead received answered inside the SLA.
- At least one real co-marketing pickup between two pilot agents.
- Zero critical blockers open; zero confidentiality incidents.
- BM/ID business-critical screens signed off.
- Security checklist evidenced.

## Expansion recommendation

- **Expand to 20–25 agents** in week 2 only if all success criteria are met and the
  scorecard targets are hit or explained.
- **Hold at 5** if any critical blocker is open, or if fewer than 3 agents were active
  ≥3 days (an adoption problem, not a code problem; fix onboarding first).
- **Fix first** if a confidentiality or money issue appears, regardless of other results.
- Provider key decision: if agents report the rule-based drafts are not useful, trial one
  provider key on staging first, with the quota caps already in `ai_settings`, before
  any production key.
